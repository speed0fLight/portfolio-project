Featured project

Reverse Engineering the Mars Rover

Integrated Mechatronics and Additive Manufacturing · Jun 2026 – Present · NASA CTSGC · CT State Naugatuck Valley

This is a NASA CTSGC Student Project funded by NASA through the CT Space Consortium. As Lead Engineer, I'm reverse engineering a Mars rover through integrated mechatronics and additive manufacturing. The project involves the following:

  • Designing a rocker-bogie suspension system and a three-bevel-gear differential assembly in Onshape.
  • Architecting the power electronics from LiPo battery to Raspberry Pi 5 and servo control.
  • Building on an earlier NASA rover prototype project (Jul 2025 – Jun 2026).
  • Mini solar panels that harvest energy to power non-essential systems (headlights, etc.)

About

I'm a Computer Science student at Naugatuck Valley Community College, transferring to Central Connecticut State University for a B.S. in Computer Science with a Cybersecurity concentration, with my sights set on the Accelerate Central BS+MS program.

My technical foundation spans Python, JavaScript, SQL, and Docker, with hands-on embedded systems work on ESP32 and Raspberry Pi platforms, and reverse engineering using Ghidra. I hold CompTIA Network+ (Security+ in progress) and have hands-on networking experience with VLANs, DHCP, and routing.

Alongside coursework, I work as a Computer Lab Assistant, help students with STEM subjects, and serve as a Lead Engineer on NVCC's NASA-affiliated Quadcopter Program. I previously led programming for my high school's robotics team and wrote extensive long-term documentation for it.

I'm building toward a career in cyber-physical systems and defense/security engineering, with the long-term goal of founding my own security consulting practice.
