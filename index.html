Featured project

Reverse Engineering the Mars Rover

Integrated Mechatronics and Additive Manufacturing · Jun 2026 – Present · NASA CTSGC · CT State Naugatuck Valley

This is a NASA CTSGC Student Project funded by NASA through the CT Space Consortium. As Lead Engineer, I'm reverse engineering a Mars rover through integrated mechatronics and additive manufacturing. The project involves the following:

  • Designing a rocker-bogie suspension system and a three-bevel-gear differential assembly in Onshape.
  • Architecting the power electronics from LiPo battery to Raspberry Pi 5 and servo control.
  • Building on an earlier NASA rover prototype project (Jul 2025 – Jun 2026).
  • Mini solar panels that harvest energy to power non-essential systems (headlights, etc.)
  • Use of both teleoperative (human piloted) and autonomous systems.
  • Folding FPV Camera for viewing location without obstructing the LiDAR system.
  • Robotic claw for sample collection.
  • LiDAR System used for 3D-Mapping and autonomous movement.

Networking Homelab

Jan 2026 – Present · CT State Naugatuck Valley

This project began as a small computer on a raspberry pi, and slowly evolved into a sophisticated setup with enterprise architecture in mind. The lab runs on proxmox, a hypervisor for running virtual machines and containers to create services! The hardware involved is a managed switch (VLANs), a router, a dell optiplex 7050, a pdu and a server rack. Uses VMs as a jumpbox for ease of configuration. Remotely accessed using tailscale. Hosts media for streaming, much like Netflix.
